Safety

Know before anyone touches it

Results show toxicity for humans, cats and dogs, with the level and symptoms spelled out, never buried. When toxicity cannot be determined, Plantz says so instead of guessing.

Do not eat or handle a plant based on AI identification alone.

Misidentification can be harmful or fatal. Confirm with a qualified botanist or a trusted field guide before any consumption. Plantz is an identification aid, not a medical or veterinary service.

If a person or a pet may have eaten an unknown plant, contact your local poison center or a vet right away. In the US: Poison Control 1-800-222-1222, ASPCA Animal Poison Control 888-426-4435 (a consultation fee may apply).
